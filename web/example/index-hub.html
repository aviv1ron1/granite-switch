Granite Switch — loaded from the HF Hub, ON transformers.js

Loads a packaged Granite Switch ONNX model directly from a Hugging Face repo via @huggingface/transformers's AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(repoId). transformers.js fetches config.json + onnx/model_<dtype>.onnx and owns the session; our loop threads the switch's per-token adapter state.
